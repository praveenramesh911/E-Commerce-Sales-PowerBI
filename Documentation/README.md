**# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard**



\## Project Overview



An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze e-commerce sales performance and provide business insights across sales, profit, quantity, products, categories, regions, and shipping types.



The dashboard uses DAX measures, time-intelligence calculations, data modeling, and interactive visualizations to support business performance analysis.



\## Business Objectives



\- Monitor overall sales performance

\- Analyze Year-to-Date (YTD) sales, profit, and quantity

\- Compare current performance with the previous year

\- Analyze Year-over-Year (YoY) performance

\- Identify top-performing products

\- Identify bottom-performing products

\- Compare sales across product categories

\- Analyze regional sales performance

\- Analyze sales by shipping type

\- Track profit margin

\- Provide an interactive dashboard for business analysis



\## Key KPIs



\- YTD Sales

\- YTD Profit

\- YTD Quantity

\- YTD Profit Margin

\- PYTD Sales

\- PYTD Profit

\- PYTD Quantity

\- YoY Sales

\- YoY Profit

\- YoY Quantity

\- YoY Profit Margin



\## Dashboard Features



\### Sales by Category

Compares sales performance across:

\- Furniture

\- Office Supplies

\- Technology



The analysis includes YTD Sales, PYTD Sales, YoY Sales, and trend indicators.



\### Top 5 Products

Displays the five products with the highest YTD sales.



\### Bottom 5 Products

Displays the five products with the lowest YTD sales.



\### Sales by Region

Provides a geographical view of sales performance across customer regions.



\### Sales by Shipping Type

Shows the distribution of sales across different shipping types.



\### Customer Segment

Allows analysis based on customer segments such as Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office.



\## Data Model



The project contains:



\- E-commerce sales data

\- Calendar table

\- US state geographical reference table



The Calendar table is connected to the e-commerce data using the Order Date field.



The geographical reference table supports map-based analysis.



\## Tools \& Technologies



\- Microsoft Power BI Desktop

\## DAX \& Time Intelligence



The dashboard uses DAX measures for:



\- YTD calculations

\- PYTD calculations

\- YoY calculations

\- Profit Margin

\- Trend indicators

\- Sales, Profit, and Quantity analysis



Time-intelligence calculations are based on the dedicated Calendar table.



\## Key Visualizations



\- KPI Cards

\- Tables

\- Bar Charts

\- Donut Charts

\- Map Visualization

\- Trend Indicators

\- Interactive Slicers



\## Interactivity



Users can interact with the dashboard using filters and slicers to analyze performance by customer segment and other business dimensions.
